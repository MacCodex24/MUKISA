Who We Are

About Us

Mac Applic International School is dedicated to providing quality education that prepares students for a global future. Our experienced staff and modern facilities ensure every learner reaches their full potential.

Founded with a vision of inclusive and innovative learning, we combine academic excellence with character development, technology, and cultural appreciation.

Our Mission

To inspire and empower every student to achieve their highest academic and personal potential through innovative, inclusive, and globally-minded education.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the leading institution in Somalia and East Africa, recognized for academic excellence, technological innovation, and holistic student development.

Our Values
